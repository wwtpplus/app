About

Process Operator Handbook is an offline handbook for wastewater treatment operators, process engineers and students. It has process calculators with the working shown, a symptom-based troubleshooting guide, typical operating ranges and an exam-practice quiz, in SI or US units. Everything runs on your device; the app collects no data.

How to use it

  • Calculate: overwrite the example values with your readings. Results, checks and the full working update as you type. Your entries are remembered on this device.
  • Plant: set up your plant once. Its volumes and areas fill the calculators, daily rounds are checked against your own limits, and trends show how things are moving.
  • Diagnose: pick a symptom to see likely causes, what to check and what to do.
  • Ranges: typical values to compare your plant against.
  • Quiz: exam practice by topic, timed mock exams, unlimited maths drills and a review of your mistakes.
  • Search: the search box at the top finds any calculator, symptom or range, offline.
  • Favourites: tap ☆ on a calculator to pin it to the top of the list.
  • Abbreviations: tap any dotted-underlined abbreviation (such as SRT) to see what it means, or open the full glossary.
  • Starter calculators: pick your role again to pin a different starter set. Choose →
  • Formulas and theory: the principle behind every calculator, its assumptions and limits, and tips for using it on a real plant. Open →
  • Logbook: tap “Save to log” on a calculator to keep a dated record of your readings and results.
