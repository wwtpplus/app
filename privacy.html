Privacy Policy

Process Operator Handbook · Last updated: 25 October 2026

This app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or usage data.

Data on your device

Values you type into the calculators, your plant details and daily readings, results you save to the logbook, quiz progress and your settings are saved only in your device's local app storage so they are there next time you open the app. They never leave your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes them.

Network use

The app works offline. When a connection is available it may download fonts from Google Fonts; Google may receive your IP address as part of that standard web request. No other network requests are made.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through the WWTP Plus online shop (wwtpplus.com), which runs on Shopify. Your order and payment details are handled by the shop and Shopify, not by this app, and the shop's privacy policy applies to the purchase. Pro is unlocked with a code you enter in the app; the code is checked on your device and is not sent anywhere.

No accounts, analytics or advertising

The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Children

The app is intended for wastewater professionals and students and is not directed at children.
